1 ◆ Introduction to Entrepreneurship

Entrepreneurship: The process of starting and managing a business venture by identifying opportunities, taking risks and creating value through innovation.
  • Creates innovative products and services.
  • Transforms ideas into business opportunities.
  • Requires creativity, leadership and managerial skills.
  • Contributes to economic growth.
Entrepreneurs act as innovators, creators, organizers and risk takers.

2 ◆ Importance of Entrepreneurship

  • Creation of job opportunities.
  • Creation of new businesses.
  • Encourages innovation.
  • Improves standard of living.
  • Supports research and development.
  • Promotes community development.
  • Increases productivity.
  • Creates national wealth.
  • Contributes to social welfare.

3 ◆ Types of Entrepreneurs

Type Description
Innovative Introduces new products and technologies.
Imitative Adopts and improves existing innovations.
Fabian Cautious and reluctant to adopt change.
Inventor Creates completely new products.
Online Operates internet-based businesses.
Home Business Runs business from home.

  2. Only good ideas matter.
  3. Business guarantees freedom.
  4. Entrepreneurship creates quick wealth.
  5. Money is the only motivator.
  6. Businesses either succeed or fail.
  7. Everything depends on entrepreneur.
  8. There is a secret formula for success.
