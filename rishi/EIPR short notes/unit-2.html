1 ◆ Identifying Market Opportunities and Trends

Business Opportunity: A favorable situation that creates a need for a product or service and has the potential to generate profit.
  • Visible only to an alert entrepreneur.
  • Requires active search and analysis.
  • Must have market potential.
  • Must generate return on investment.
  • Should be stable, realistic and commercially viable.
Market Need = Future Demand − Future Supply

2 ◆ Opportunity Assessment Plan

Feature Opportunity Assessment Plan Business Plan
Length Short Lengthy
Focus Idea / Opportunity Complete Venture
Financial Statements No Yes
Purpose Evaluate Opportunity Guide Implementation

Major Section 1 - Product/Service Idea

  • Market need addressed
  • Product features
  • Competitive products
  • Existing companies
  • Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

Major Section 2 - Market

  • Market size
  • Growth trends
  • Domestic and global opportunities
  • Market growth rate

Ideation is a structured process of generating, evaluating and refining ideas.

Stages of Ideation

Stage Description
Problem Identification Define challenge or opportunity
Idea Generation Brainstorm solutions
Idea Evaluation Filter ideas using feasibility criteria
Implementation Planning Create execution strategy

6 ◆ Technical Feasibility: PoC & Prototype Development

Proof of Concept (PoC): Demonstrates whether an idea is technically feasible.

Why PoC?

  • Feasibility testing
  • Risk reduction
  • Investor confidence
  • Validation of concept

Prototype Types

Type Description
Low Fidelity Paper sketches and mockups
High Fidelity Near-final realistic models
Digital Software simulations
